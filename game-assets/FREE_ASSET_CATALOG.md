# Free World Maps, Audio & VFX Asset Catalog

BizXtreme uses openly licensed external assets instead of generated artwork. The catalog favors CC0/public-domain sources so realistic and nostalgic maps, sound effects and VFX can be reused across WebGL, Unity, Unreal, desktop and mobile targets.

## Realistic map/environment sources

- **Poly Haven** — CC0 3D models, HDRIs and photoscanned PBR textures for next-generation environments.
- **Poly Haven Rocky Terrain / Rocks Ground** — CC0 terrain materials with diffuse, displacement, normal and roughness maps.
- **OpenGameArt 180+ Seamless Hex Tiles** — CC0 realistic desert, volcanic, forest, lava, grass, dirt, water and rocky terrain tiles.
- **OpenGameArt Open World Tileset** — CC0 HD top-down open-world terrain, roads, water, elevation and buildings.

## Nostalgic map sources

- **Kenney Map Pack** — CC0 overworld, terrain, roads and landscape tiles.
- **Kenney Cartography Pack** — CC0 fantasy map tiles, icons, vectors and paper textures.
- **Kenney Minimap Pack** — CC0 minimap tiles in six styles.
- **Puny World Tileset** — CC0 16x16 old-school RPG overworld with grass, rivers, sea, buildings and resources.
- **Isometric Pixel Terrain** — CC0 isometric terrain and animated water.
- **Overworld Map** — CC0 retro desert/grass/dirt/cliff/water map material.

## Audio sources

- **Kenney 50 RPG Sound Effects** — CC0 footsteps, metal, cloth, books, coins and interactions.
- **OwlishMedia Sound Effects Pack** — CC0 ambience, footsteps, impacts, water, UI, sci-fi and technology sounds.
- **LEGIT Audio The Shop Free SFX** — CC0 store ambience, room tones, drones, appliances and engines.
- **OpenGameArt Free Sounds** — free/CC0 sound collections; preserve individual license/credit metadata.

## VFX sources

- **70 Animated 2D Game Effects** — CC0 animated particle/effect sheets.
- **Lots of Game Effects** — CC0 aura, fire, blast and explosion spritesheets.
- **PVFX Foundry** — CC0 combat, elemental, defensive, support, portal, beam, water and environmental pixel VFX.
- **itch.io CC0 Effects catalog** — large CC0 effects catalog with sprites, textures, tilesets and audio.

## BizXtreme world families

The common world registry should cover alpine, desert, Mediterranean, forest, tropical, coast, island, river valley, mountain, volcanic, snowy, grassland, farmland, industrial city, modern city, harbor, ancient ruins, fantasy, space colony and retro RPG worlds. Each world supports day/night, weather, material variants and retro rendering where practical.

Use licensed building blocks to construct original game maps rather than copying copyrighted complete maps. Procedural map generation should produce the final game-specific layouts.

## Asset rules

1. Prefer CC0/public-domain assets for bundled redistribution.
2. Record attribution for CC-BY and other attribution-required assets.
3. Reject unclear, missing or incompatible licenses.
4. Do not put private/proprietary assets into public builds.
5. Keep original source URLs and license information beside every imported asset.
